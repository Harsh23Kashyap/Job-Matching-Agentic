We submit the manuscript \textbf{``JobMatch: An Agentic Multi-Role Platform for Explainable Job--Candidate Matching''} for consideration as an \textbf{original research regular paper} in the \textit{Journal of Autonomous Agents and Multi-Agent Systems}. The work has not been published previously in archival form and is not under consideration elsewhere.

The paper presents JobMatch, a role-aware multi-agent hiring platform in which Candidate, Employer, and Matchmaking agents coordinate profile ingestion, embedding, and read-only hybrid scoring across separate portals. We believe the manuscript fits JAAMAS because it studies \textbf{autonomous and multi-agent system design} in a deployed setting: agents have distinct responsibilities, coordinate through a shared event bus, and support \textbf{agentic hiring workflows} while keeping hiring decisions with human users. The Matchmaking agent ranks and explains candidate--job pairs using a composite model (semantic, skill, and constraint signals) rather than acting autonomously on behalf of recruiters or applicants. We pair this architecture with an \textbf{empirical offline evaluation} on a labeled corpus of 30 resumes, 15 job postings, and 47 graded relevance pairs, comparing lexical, dense, fusion, and reranking methods with metrics reported from committed benchmark artifacts (e.g., nDCG@5 of 0.969 and 0.968 for the best research drivers; composite ablation nDCG@5 of 0.942). We document an integrated research prototype with reproducible benchmarks tied to deployed code paths, not as a validated production hiring system.
The manuscript follows the structure: Introduction, Literature Review, System Architecture, Methodology, Architectural Realization, Evaluation Framework, Results and Discussion, Conclusion, Future Work, and Declarations.

This manuscript has \textbf{not been published previously} in any archival venue, preprint server, or technical report presenting the full paper, and no portion of the text has appeared as a colored-layout preprint. \textbf{All authors have reviewed the manuscript and approved this submission.} We declare no competing interests relevant to this work. Data and code availability statements are included in the manuscript declarations.
