Dear Editors,

We submit the manuscript **"JobMatch: An Agentic Multi-Role Platform for Explainable Job–Candidate Matching"** for consideration as an **original research regular paper** in the *Journal of Autonomous Agents and Multi-Agent Systems*. The work has not been published previously in archival form and is not under consideration elsewhere.

The paper presents JobMatch, a role-aware multi-agent hiring platform in which Candidate, Employer, and Matchmaking agents coordinate profile ingestion, embedding, and read-only hybrid scoring across separate portals. We believe the manuscript fits JAAMAS because it studies **autonomous and multi-agent system design** in a deployed setting: agents have distinct responsibilities, coordinate through a shared event bus, and support **agentic hiring workflows** while keeping hiring decisions with human users. The Matchmaking agent ranks and explains candidate–job pairs using a composite model (semantic, skill, and constraint signals) rather than acting autonomously on behalf of recruiters or applicants. We pair this architecture with an **empirical offline evaluation** on a labeled corpus of 30 resumes, 15 job postings, and 47 graded relevance pairs, comparing lexical, dense, fusion, and reranking methods with metrics reported from committed benchmark artifacts (the portal-default composite reaches nDCG@5 of 0.949; on this small corpus, n=30, no method is statistically distinguishable from the others, so we report ranking parity rather than superiority). We present the contribution as an integrated research prototype with reproducible benchmarks, not as a validated production hiring system.

This manuscript has **not been published previously** in any archival venue, preprint server, or technical report presenting the full paper, and no portion of the text has appeared as a colored-layout preprint. **All authors have reviewed the manuscript and approved this submission.** We declare no competing interests relevant to this work. Data and code availability statements are included in the manuscript declarations.
