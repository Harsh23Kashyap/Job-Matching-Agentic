\title[JobMatch: Agentic Explainable Job--Candidate Matching]{JobMatch: An Agentic Multi-Role Platform for Explainable Job--Candidate Matching}

\abstract{Job seekers and recruiters frequently receive ranked lists without clear attribution of profile ownership, scoring rationale, or control over consequential actions.
We present JobMatch, a multi-agent recruitment platform in which Candidate and Employer agents maintain role-specific profiles and embeddings, while a read-only Matchmaking Agent scores and explains candidate--job pairs.
Agents coordinate through an event bus; when a resume or job description changes, the owning agent refreshes vector representations and the Matchmaking layer drops stale rankings.
Separate portals keep save, apply, and shortlist decisions with human users.

The Matchmaking agent combines semantic similarity, skill overlap, and experience, compensation, and remote compatibility into a fixed composite score with structured explanations---assistive ranking, not autonomous hiring.
We evaluate offline on 30 resumes, 15 job postings, and 47 graded relevance pairs using macro-averaged P@5, R@5, and nDCG@5 at $K{=}5$, comparing TF--IDF, BM25, dense retrieval, reciprocal rank fusion, learned fusion, and cross-encoder reranking.
Multimodal soft-embed and learned fusion reach nDCG@5 of 0.969 and 0.968 in committed artifacts; composite ablation reaches 0.949 under the portal-default weight vector.
Cross-encoder reranking reduces nDCG@5 by 0.108 at ${\sim}141.7$ ms/query and stays disabled in the default portal.
Hard-negative mining yields 150 pairs with zero label conflicts; a synthetic fairness audit flags 7/10 fabricated pairs---an engineering check, not demographic validation.
Benchmark drivers and regression tests reproduce reported metrics on an integrated research prototype, not a validated production hiring system.}

\keywords{multi-agent systems, recruitment platforms, explainable matching, job--candidate ranking, hybrid retrieval}
