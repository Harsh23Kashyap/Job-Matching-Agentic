## Verdict

**The current ESWA manuscript would be desk-rejected or rejected for research-integrity
reasons, not merely presentation.** The good news, established by the audit: the honest
replacements for most broken numbers **already exist on disk** (`backend/reports/extended_evaluation/*`,
generated by `extended_evaluation.py`, docstring "Extended evaluation suite for ESWA
submission") — they are simply uncommitted, uncited, and contradicted by the numbers the
`.tex` still prints. The core composite result (nDCG@5 = 0.949) **survives** held-out
validation. So the path is: replace leaked/phantom numbers with the honest held-out ones,
reframe the claims to what the evidence supports, expand the benchmark, and re-QA.

Overall readiness now: **NOT SUBMITTABLE.** 12 BLOCKER-class issues, ~15 HIGH.
